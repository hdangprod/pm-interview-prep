# Roadmap: September 10–16

[Home](README.md) · [Quick review](QUICK_REVIEW.md) · [Gemini prompt](gemini/COACH_PROMPT.md)

Choose the nearest useful day; the dates are direction, not gates. Skip familiar material, slow down when you cannot explain it, and let a case reveal what to revisit. No catch-up reports or required study hours.

## September 10 — Product thinking and customer discovery

- **Goal:** Understand a user's problem before proposing a feature.
- **Core concepts:** [Problem, priority, specs and metrics](study/PRODUCT_THINKING.md); [discovery, personas and JTBD](study/CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY.md).
- **By the end:** Explain whose problem matters, ask about a real incident, write a clear problem statement, and propose one small validation.
- **Suggested Gemini practice:** Paste the coach prompt and say “Teach me customer discovery, then give me Katalon case K-F01.” Answer aloud before asking for feedback.
- **Optional deeper study:** Use the [evidence ledger](stories/evidence-ledger.md) to recall one true integration incident: your action, observable result and what you did not measure.

## September 11 — AI, agents and MCP

- **Goal:** Explain the system and justify the amount of intelligence and autonomy it needs.
- **Core concepts:** [AI → context → tools → agents → MCP → control](study/AI_AGENTS_MCP.md).
- **By the end:** Distinguish a chatbot, workflow and agent; trace an MCP request; explain when approval is needed and when AI adds little value.
- **Suggested Gemini practice:** “Explain MCP simply, then test me.” Next, explain one agent decision aloud without notes.
- **Optional deeper study:** Sketch a real PRJ226 interaction from memory. Mark uncertain details; do not retrofit an architecture that was never implemented.

## September 12 — QA workflows and AI product judgment

- **Goal:** Find useful AI opportunities across a tester's job.
- **Core concepts:** [Testing workflow](study/TESTING_FOR_PM.md); [AI evaluation and trade-offs](study/AI_AGENTS_MCP.md#6-evaluate-the-users-job).
- **By the end:** Distinguish product defects from bad tests; explain why a passing generated test may be useless; propose one evaluation and a bounded feature spec.
- **Suggested Gemini practice:** Choose [K-D01 or K-C03](katalon/CASES.md#intermediate). “Challenge my metrics, then give me a constraint.”
- **Optional deeper study:** Read one relevant capability in [Katalon research](katalon/research.md), then explain the customer benefit and limitation. Do not memorize the catalog.

## September 13 — Marketplace mechanics

- **Goal:** Follow a decision through buyer, merchant, driver and platform outcomes.
- **Core concepts:** [Order lifecycle, liquidity, capacity and economics](study/MARKETPLACE_FOR_PM.md).
- **By the end:** Explain a promotion's first- and second-order effects, separate GMV/revenue/contribution, and diagnose a local supply bottleneck.
- **Suggested Gemini practice:** “Teach me food-delivery liquidity with one example, then test me.” Follow with a [foundation case](shopeefood/CASES.md#foundation).
- **Optional deeper study:** Observe one actual ordering flow and separate what you can see from assumptions about matching or pricing.

## September 14 — Marketplace business cases

- **Goal:** Move from ambiguous numbers to a practical recommendation.
- **Core concepts:** [Order-decline reflex](study/MARKETPLACE_FOR_PM.md#diagnose-an-order-decline); segmentation, unit economics, prioritization and experiment spillovers.
- **By the end:** Define the affected metric, request decisive data, rank explanations, recommend one action and explain when to stop or change it.
- **Suggested Gemini practice:** Take [S-D01](shopeefood/CASES.md#intermediate), then one pressure case. Ask Gemini to release consistent hypothetical data only when requested.
- **Optional deeper study:** Retry with half the budget or a different city/time segment; see whether your recommendation changes.

## September 15 — Integrated mocks and pressure

- **Goal:** Explain sound decisions under questioning in both interview settings.
- **Core concepts:** Evidence, judgment, clear recommendations and honest ownership.
- **By the end:** Complete one mock per track; defend a recommendation through follow-ups; tell a true story without exaggeration; identify your own next improvement.
- **Suggested Gemini practice:** “Run a Katalon mock” and “Run a ShopeeFood mock” using the [case-bank mock prompts](katalon/CASES.md#mock-interview) and [business assessment prompt](shopeefood/CASES.md#mock-interview). One question at a time.
- **Optional deeper study:** Repeat only the hardest moment. Use [phrases](english/phrase-bank.md) to say the same reasoning more clearly, not to memorize a script.

## September 16 — Light review

- **Goal:** Recall what you know and arrive ready to think.
- **Core concepts:** [Quick-review reflexes](QUICK_REVIEW.md) and your actual experience.
- **By the end:** Explain one AI judgment example and one marketplace trade-off; recall your opening and personal contribution to a true story.
- **Suggested Gemini practice:** “Light review: one short question from each track. No new topics.” Speak the answers.
- **Optional deeper study:** None needed. Check confirmed interview times, location and travel; those details are not yet in this kit.
