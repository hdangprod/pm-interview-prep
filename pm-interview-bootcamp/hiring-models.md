# Hiring models and initial hypotheses

Researched 2026-09-10. Sources: linked track research files. The following hiring bars, red flags, and interviewer perspectives are COACH INFERENCES, not confidential company rubrics.

| Dimension | Katalon PM Intern | ShopeeFood APM |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central hiring question | Can this person investigate a QA workflow and shape a useful, reliable AI-enabled product improvement? | Can this person connect user needs, business results, and operational constraints to a workable product decision? |
| Knowledge needed | Testing lifecycle, APIs, LLM/agent boundaries, MCP, evaluation, requirements | Order lifecycle, geographic/time liquidity, funnels, retention, capacity, incentives, contribution economics |
| Desired behavior | Ask precise questions, inspect AI output, challenge requests respectfully, define testable scope | Segment before concluding, quantify effects, consider all sides, recommend feasible action with trade-offs |
| Likely junior bar | Learns fast, makes assumptions explicit, can draft a small spec and validation approach with guidance | Can turn an ambiguous business problem into a defensible recommendation and collaborate with guidance |
| PM interviewer lens | Discovery questions, workflow details, acceptance criteria, how the candidate actually checks AI work | Metric definitions, data requests, operations, feature scope, implementation with business teams |
| Director lens | Why this problem matters, adoption/trust, opportunity cost, evidence, judgment about autonomy | Which business outcome matters, resource allocation, ecosystem health, economics, clarity under uncertainty |
| Likely red flags | Agent enthusiasm without verification; MCP recitation; shipping a request before understanding need | More promotions as the default; optimizing orders alone; ignoring drivers/merchants; confusing correlation and causation |

Shared bar: user research, product/data reasoning, clear requirements, cross-functional communication, and honest ownership. The roles differ in emphasis; Katalon also needs business judgment and ShopeeFood also needs discovery/specification.

## Candidate hypotheses, not demonstrated findings

- Likely strengths: integration troubleshooting may transfer to decomposition; customer-facing work may transfer to discovery and stakeholder explanation; AI usage may provide unusually concrete junior PM evidence.
- Likely weaknesses to test: moving from technical fix to validated customer outcome; meaningful denominators and causal tests; operational marketplace reasoning; explaining AI failures and MCP beyond vocabulary; concise English.
- Biggest risks: unverified project claims, polished stories collapsing under probing, solutions before diagnosis, and optimizing one metric in isolation.
- Differentiators to extract: a real customer problem where the initial request was misleading; a real PRJ226/AI incident where the candidate checked output and changed course.
- Disconfirm strength hypotheses if answers cannot establish personal decisions or evidence. Do not rate CV keywords as competence.
